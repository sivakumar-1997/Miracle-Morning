Miracle Mornings Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026 • Contact: support@miraclemornings.app

1. Overview

Miracle Mornings ('we', 'our', or 'the App') respects your privacy and is designed as a peaceful daily mindfulness, sound-relaxation, and gratitude companion across Android and Web. This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored locally, what is synced if you opt into an account, and how your choices are protected.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

By default, your daily morning and night progress—including your 28-day 'The Magic' lesson day, 10 daily gratitude blessings, 8-sip water intention & count, night reflections, streak calendar dates, and timezone formatting—is stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences (on Android) or browser localStorage (on Web).

3. Optional Account & Cloud Synchronization

Signing in is optional. If you choose to create an account or sign in for multi-device synchronization, we collect and store your email address, encrypted authentication session tokens, and your manifestation records (streak count, completed dates, water intentions, gratitude blessings, night reflections, and session timestamps) in our Supabase cloud database solely to keep your Android and Web devices in sync.

4. Permissions, Audio & Device Sensors

Miracle Mornings follows a strict least-privilege approach. The app uses standard network access only for optional account sign-in and cloud sync. All 432 Hz ambient soundscapes, 7-chakra Solfeggio tones (396 Hz–963 Hz), and Ho'oponopono guidance are synthesized on-device or spoken via your browser/device Text-to-Speech engine. We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, or precise GPS location.

5. No Selling of Personal Data & No Third-Party Ads

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal reflections, gratitude lists, or email address to advertisers or data brokers. The app contains no third-party behavioral ad trackers.

6. Data Retention, Deletion & Your Rights

You may use Miracle Mornings locally without creating an account, sign out at any time, or clear local app/browser storage to erase local records. If you have created a synced account and wish to export or permanently delete your account and cloud records, email us at support@miraclemornings.app.

7. Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

Miracle Mornings is intended for users aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. We may update this Privacy Policy as features evolve; any changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date above.
